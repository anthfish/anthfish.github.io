// 01 — Professional Background

Profile

Anthony Fisher is an IT support and cybersecurity professional with a foundation in customer-facing sales. His professional formation took place in a family-owned Ford dealership operated for over sixty years — an environment that instilled the disciplines of client assessment, trust-building, and direct problem resolution.

In late 2024, he transitioned into cybersecurity. The troubleshooting instinct was already present; the transition applied it to systems that compound in value. He holds CompTIA Network+ and Security+ certifications and maintains two documented home laboratories.

Anthony is currently open to entry-level IT support and SOC engagements. His distinguishing asset is not technical alone: it is the demonstrated ability to manage a frustrated user, de-escalate promptly, and close a ticket cleanly — technically and experientially.
